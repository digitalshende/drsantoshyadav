Recovery & Rehabilitation

What to expect before and after joint surgery: preparation, physiotherapy and safe return to routine.

Overview

Good surgery is only half of the result. Preparation beforehand and steady rehabilitation afterwards decide how quickly you regain confidence and movement.

Common signs

  • Before surgery: pre-operative tests, medicine review and home preparation
  • Right after surgery: pain control, wound care and early walking
  • Weeks that follow: physiotherapy, swelling control and gradual progress
  • Long term: weight management, strength work and scheduled review visits

What the process looks like

  1. PrepareArrange a chair with arms, a clear walking path and a helper at home.
  2. Move earlyShort, supervised walks reduce stiffness and clot risk.
  3. Do your exercisesDaily physiotherapy matters more than any single visit.
  4. ReviewAttend follow-ups and report fever, spreading redness or sudden swelling promptly.

Common questions

When can I drive?

It depends on the operated side and your control of the limb. Ask at your review visit.

Are exercises painful?

Some soreness is expected, but sharp or worsening pain should be reported.

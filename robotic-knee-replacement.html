Robotic Knee Replacement

Robotic-assisted total, partial and bilateral knee replacement in Satara by Dr Santosh Yadav.

Overview

When walking, stairs and sleep are limited by worn-out knee joints, a replacement can restore comfortable movement. Robotic assistance helps Dr. Yadav plan bone cuts and implant position on a 3D model of your own knee before and during surgery.

Common signs

  • Daily pain that continues despite medicines, injections and exercises
  • X-rays showing advanced cartilage loss or deformity (bow-legs or knock-knees)
  • Difficulty walking short distances, climbing stairs or getting up from a chair
  • Both knees affected, where a bilateral procedure may be considered after fitness checks

What the process looks like

  1. AssessmentClinical examination, standing X-rays and a review of your medical conditions and medicines.
  2. PlanningA personalised plan for implant size, alignment and soft-tissue balance.
  3. SurgeryThe robotic system guides precise bone preparation while the surgeon controls every step.
  4. Early walkingSupervised physiotherapy begins soon after surgery, with a home exercise plan at discharge.

Common questions

How long does recovery take?

Most people walk with support within a day or two. Return to routine activity varies with age, fitness and physiotherapy, and your surgeon will give you a personal timeline.

Can both knees be done together?

In suitable patients a bilateral replacement may be possible. Eligibility depends on heart, lung and general health, which are checked first.

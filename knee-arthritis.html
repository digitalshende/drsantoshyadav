Knee Arthritis & Joint Pain

Understand knee arthritis and the treatment ladder from lifestyle and injections to replacement.

Overview

Arthritis wears away the cushioning cartilage in the knee, causing pain, swelling and stiffness. Not everyone needs surgery. Treatment follows a ladder, and you move up only when simpler steps stop working.

Common signs

  • Pain that increases with walking, stairs or squatting
  • Morning stiffness and swelling
  • A grinding sensation or visible change in leg shape
  • Reduced distance you can walk comfortably

What the process looks like

  1. Lifestyle & weightWeight loss and low-impact activity reduce load on the joint.
  2. Physiotherapy & medicinesStrengthening and carefully chosen medicines ease symptoms.
  3. InjectionsSelected patients may benefit from joint injections after discussion.
  4. Joint replacementConsidered when pain and function stay poor despite these measures.

Common questions

Can arthritis be reversed?

Cartilage does not grow back fully, but symptoms can often be controlled for years.

When is surgery the right choice?

When pain disrupts sleep, walking and daily life despite proper non-surgical care.

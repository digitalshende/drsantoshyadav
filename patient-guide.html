Patient Guide

Practical steps for your first visit, hospital stay and return home.

Before your visit

  • Bring previous X-rays, MRI reports and prescriptions
  • List medicines, allergies and past surgeries
  • Note when pain started and what makes it better or worse
  • Bring a family member if surgery may be discussed

Before surgery

  • Complete advised blood tests, heart and chest checks
  • Follow fasting and medicine instructions exactly
  • Prepare a safe, uncluttered home with seating at the right height
  • Arrange help for the first few weeks

Call us promptly if you notice

  • Fever or chills after surgery
  • Redness, heat or discharge at the wound
  • Sudden increase in calf pain or swelling
  • Chest pain or breathlessness (seek emergency care)

Health Library

Short, plain-language articles on bone and joint health.

Knee pain after 40: when to see an orthopaedic doctor

Pain that lasts beyond a few weeks, swelling, or night pain deserves an examination. Early advice can prevent unnecessary damage.

Protecting your knees while exercising

Warm up, build strength gradually, choose supportive footwear and rest when pain sharpens.

Preparing your home for joint replacement

Remove loose rugs, add grab bars, keep essentials at waist height and plan seating with firm support.

Shin splints: understanding overuse pain

Running too much too soon is a common cause. Gradual progression and recovery days help.

Bone health through the years

Calcium, vitamin D, weight-bearing exercise and sunlight support strong bones. Ask your doctor before starting supplements.

Returning to sport after ligament surgery

Strength and movement milestones matter more than the calendar. Patience reduces re-injury risk.

ACL & PCL Ligament Reconstruction

Cruciate ligament reconstruction for twisting knee injuries, with sports-focused rehabilitation in Satara.

Overview

The cruciate ligaments keep your knee stable. After a twisting injury or collision, a torn ACL or PCL can leave the knee giving way, which may damage cartilage over time. Reconstruction rebuilds the ligament with a graft.

Common signs

  • Knee gives way during pivoting or sudden stops
  • A popping sensation and swelling at the time of injury
  • Combined ligament and meniscus injuries
  • Athletes or active people who want to return to sport

What the process looks like

  1. Confirm the injuryMRI and stability tests show which ligaments and cartilage are involved.
  2. Pre-habilitationReducing swelling and restoring movement before surgery improves results.
  3. Arthroscopic reconstructionA graft is positioned through small incisions using camera guidance.
  4. Phased rehabStrength, balance and agility work progress step by step over months.

Common questions

Does every torn ACL need surgery?

No. Lower-demand patients may do well with rehabilitation. Surgery is usually advised when the knee stays unstable.

What graft is used?

Graft choice is discussed based on your age, sport and anatomy.

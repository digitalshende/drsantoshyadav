Arthroscopy & Sports Injury Care

Keyhole knee and shoulder surgery and sports injury treatment for athletes and active people in Satara.

Overview

Arthroscopy uses a thin camera and small instruments through tiny cuts, so joint problems can be seen and repaired with less tissue damage than open surgery. It suits both competitive players and weekend runners.

Common signs

  • Meniscus tears causing catching, locking or swelling
  • Cartilage injuries and loose fragments inside the joint
  • Shoulder instability, rotator cuff problems and impingement
  • Sports injuries that do not settle with rest and rehabilitation

What the process looks like

  1. ExaminationMovement tests, X-ray and MRI identify what is injured.
  2. Non-surgical trialRest, bracing, physiotherapy and medicines are tried first when appropriate.
  3. Keyhole procedureRepair or trimming is performed through small incisions, often as a short-stay procedure.
  4. Return to sportA staged programme guides you back to training and match play.

Common questions

Is arthroscopy done as day surgery?

Many procedures need only a short hospital stay; your surgeon will confirm for your case.

When can I play again?

It depends on the injury and repair type. Returning too early risks re-injury, so clearance follows physical milestones.

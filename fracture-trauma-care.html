Fracture & Trauma Care

Fracture fixation and complex trauma care with 24-hour hospital services in Satara.

Overview

Road accidents, falls and sports collisions can break bones in many ways. Prompt assessment and stable fixation help bones heal in good alignment and allow early movement.

Common signs

  • Suspected fracture after a fall or accident
  • Pain, deformity or inability to bear weight
  • Open wounds near a broken bone
  • Fractures that failed to heal or healed in a poor position

What the process looks like

  1. Emergency carePain relief, splinting and imaging are arranged as soon as you arrive.
  2. Treatment decisionCasting or surgery with plates, rods or screws, depending on the fracture.
  3. Surgery if neededFixation aims for stability so the limb can start moving early.
  4. Follow-upCheck X-rays and physiotherapy guide healing and return to work.

Common questions

Is the hospital open at night?

Balaji Shriratna Hospital offers 24-hour services. For emergencies, call ahead on the numbers below.

Do all fractures need surgery?

No. Many heal well in a cast or brace; surgery is for unstable or displaced fractures.

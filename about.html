About Dr. Santosh Yadav

An orthopaedic surgeon focused on joint replacement, arthroscopy and sports injuries.

Seventeen years of care in Satara

Dr. Santosh Yadav has treated patients in Satara for over 17 years and has performed more than 3,000 joint replacement surgeries. His practice centres on robotic total and bilateral knee replacement, hip arthroplasty, ACL and PCL reconstruction and complex fracture care.

He trained in Solapur and Mumbai, then travelled abroad to learn techniques in joint replacement, arthroscopy and sports medicine, so patients in Satara can access them close to home.

How he practises
